THE UNIVERSAL HIVE — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026 · Operator: THE UNIVERSAL HIVE · Contact: jackschitt1134@gmail.com

The public site

The public Hive site is static pages. There are no accounts and no sign-ups on the public site, and we run no advertising trackers on it. Our host (GitHub Pages) keeps standard technical logs, the way nearly every host does.

KRACKERJACK AI, the site guide

The guide runs in your browser. In site-guide mode, your questions are answered on your own device from the site's own published data (the app and agent registries) — they are not transmitted to us or stored.

Contact and the Foundry

If you write to us, or reach out through the App Foundry door, we receive what you send (such as your name, contact details, and your project description) and use it only to respond and to do the work you asked about.

Apps the Hive builds

Each app the Foundry builds for someone carries that app's own privacy notice, written for what that app actually does. This policy covers the Hive's own sites and services.

The Quant Desk

The Hive Quant Desk is a paper-trading research tool. It trades no real money and holds no one's financial credentials.

What we never do

We never sell personal information. We share it only with processors a service cannot run without, or when the law genuinely requires it. And as a matter of founding policy: the Hive does not harvest its builders. What you bring to the Hive stays yours.

Your choices

Ask what we hold about you, or ask for correction or deletion, at the contact email above.

Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new effective date.

These are our working documents, written in plain language. They are not legal advice.
