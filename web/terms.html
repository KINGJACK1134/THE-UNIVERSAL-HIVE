The promise

The Hive exists to make things: it only needs you to want to do something — small or huge — and it works to make it, because anything is possible and nothing is out of reach unless you're not reaching for it.

How building works — the Hive's terms

  • Free to build. Having the Hive build your app, tool, or site costs nothing up front.
  • The Hive collects only on success, and only when all three are true: the work is real (it works), marketed (it is out in the world), and making money. Then the Hive collects on a low, easy, affordable scale. Nothing is owed before all three hold.
  • The split, stated plainly. The idea is yours — it belongs to the person who directed the build, and the Hive never claims it. The work and the product are the Hive's — they belong to the creator of the Hive, the same way a manufacturer's work belongs to the manufacturer who made it. Both halves of that split are stated here, up front, before anyone builds. Nothing about it is hidden, and nothing about it takes your idea from you.
  • Two honest ways the product becomes yours. First: buy the finished product. The Hive builds working products meant to make money, and its stated aim is to bring the person with the idea to the point where they can buy the manufactured product from the Hive — the same as buying a finished product from any maker who built it for you. Second: buy it out. Attain the work itself outright, by agreement, priced under the model stated on this page — low, easy, affordable — whether or not the work has gone out into the world yet. When a buyout is complete, the built work is yours outright and the Hive keeps no claim on it. Neither path will be moved after the fact: the terms in force when you build are the terms under which you can attain what was built.

The Hive will not use what you bring, or what it creates for you, for monetary gain in any way other than exactly what this page states: collection on success — real, marketed, and making money, on the low, easy, affordable scale — or a buyout. The Hive does not sell, license, or train on builders' work or ideas, and takes no right in them that is not stated on this page. The stated model is the whole deal: nothing is taken that was not clearly agreed here.
  • Little or no upfront money, honestly stated. The Hive goes to the extreme to build for little or no money up front — and 'no money up front' is the goal every single time. Sometimes a build honestly cannot be done for free: hard costs exist (domain names, government filings, third-party charges the Hive does not control). When they do, they are named in the open, in plain numbers, before any work begins — never buried, never a surprise. Little or no means exactly that: zero wherever zero is possible, little where it is not, and the difference always explained.
